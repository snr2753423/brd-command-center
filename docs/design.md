# Design

BRD Command Center is for feedlot managers comparing arrival-week plans across four yards. The mood is a technical operations console: quiet, dense, and readable at a demo distance. One accent, oxidized copper `#B85C38`, marks the priority cohort, current practice, and primary actions. Everything else is ink `#1B1E23` on `#F3F4F6`, with white surfaces. Type is the system sans stack: Segoe UI, Helvetica Neue, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif.

Screens on the storyboard are Overview, Cohort, Compare, Optimizer, and Export. Each has a loading, empty, error, and success state. Synthetic data and assumption tags stay on those screens. No sixth view.
